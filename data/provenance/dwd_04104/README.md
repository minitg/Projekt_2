# DWD station 04104 evidence

Source: Deutscher Wetterdienst (DWD), station Regensburg (04104). Original
station and parameter text files were generated by DWD on 2025-05-15 and copied
unchanged from the local historical products ending 2024-12-31. DWD data is
licensed CC BY 4.0; attribution and official dataset-documentation URLs are
recorded in `audit.json`.

`audit_dwd_metadata.py` creates:

- `audit.json`: raw-product and archive hashes, verified UTC periods, primary-data
  interval evidence, and explicit limits on publication-latency claims.
- `source_comparison.csv`: complete CSV/original-product comparison for six variables.
- `source_quality_flags.parquet`: original hourly QN procedure codes, indexed in UTC.
- `source_quality_summary.csv`: counts of QN codes over the original CSV period.
- `rain_interval_comparison.csv`: all 13 time-offset candidates on identical
  per-year support; comparison uses primary DWD ten-minute precipitation.

The audit establishes source identity and UTC for 2015–2024. It does not establish
historical receipt times, unrevised point-in-time values, or a live feed. The
inferred -10 minute rain offset is empirical evidence, not an official exact
minute guarantee. Primary experiments start in 2018 after the documented change
from PLUVIO to rain[e]H3 on 2017-11-14.
